# Open issues

What the paper does not state or what still needs a decision.

- **Delay task content is not given** [decision, `i-delay`, S1]: The paper says respondents completed unscramble and word search tasks to clear working memory; the appendix shows only a placeholder for the delay content. Neither task items nor duration are stated. Suggested: Supply the task content and duration if known; otherwise keep the replay without a delay and report it as an adaptation. (p. 5, p. 19)
- **Position and audience of the recall questions are not stated** [check, `i-recall-pos`, S1]: The recall questions are in the appendix and the footnote says a three-question battery was used, but the paper does not say where in the survey they came, and the control group was not asked the second and third questions. Suggested: Obtain the survey flow from the authors. The replay does not administer recall. (p. 7, p. 25)
- **Eligibility criteria are not reported** [check, `i-eligibility`, n-part]: The paper describes the panel but gives no eligibility or exclusion rules (the supplement's reporting checklist points to pages 2-3, which do not list them). Suggested: Ask the authors or YouGov for the eligibility rules. (p. 4)
- **Respondents per condition are not reported** [check, `i-cells`, n-part]: Only model Ns (987, 986, 764) and the 231 control respondents not asked the investigation question can be inferred; the sizes of the innuendo, denial and causal arms are not given. Suggested: Request cell sizes from the authors or the replication data. (p. 15, sources/supp_materials.txt)
- **Standard error type is not stated** [check, `i-sevariance`, a-ols]: Table 2 reports standard errors in parentheses but does not say whether they are classical or robust. Suggested: Check the replication data when posted. (p. 15)
- **Block definition is inferred** [check, `i-blocks`, d-design]: The paper says respondents were block-randomized on their answer to the crooked question but does not list the blocks. Suggested: Confirm with the authors. (p. 4, p. 19)
- **Weights cannot be reproduced** [decision, `i-weights`, v-weights]: YouGov survey weights and the block inverse probability weights depend on the panel and the realized assignment, which are not available. Suggested: Decide whether to treat unweighted OLS as the comparison model (default). (p. 6)
- **AI participant background is not specified** [decision, `i-persona`, sim-1]: The sample is described as approximately representative of the U.S. population with 35% Democrats and 27% Republicans, but the paper reports no individual-level characteristics that a prompt could use. Suggested: Decide whether AI participants should be given demographic or partisan backgrounds, and label that choice as a researcher decision. (p. 4)
- **Sample demographics are available only on request** [check, `i-demo`, n-part]: Details of the sample's demographic make-up are said to be available upon request; only party identification percentages and unweighted balance tables are printed. Suggested: Request the details from the authors. (p. 4, p. 21)
- **Response rate and participant flow are not reported** [check, `i-flow`, n-part]: The supplement's reporting checklist lists the response rate and CONSORT diagram as pending with YouGov. Suggested: Request from the authors. (sources/supp_materials.txt)
- **The senator is called Don Swensen in Table 1 and Dan Swensen in the appendix questions** [check, `i-name`, m-q-fav]: Table 1 uses 'Don Swensen' while the appendix outcome and recall questions say 'Dan Swensen'. Suggested: Confirm with the authors which spelling respondents saw in the questions. (p. 19, p. 13)
- **Order of the outcome questions differs between the text and the appendix** [check, `i-qorder`, S1]: The text introduces favorability, then bribes, then the investigation question; the appendix shows favorability, investigation, then bribes, and puts the investigation question on its own page. Suggested: Confirm the order with the authors. (p. 5, p. 20)
- **Numeric codes in the appendix response options** [check, `i-codes`, m-q-fav]: The appendix prints bracketed numbers after each option (e.g. 'Very favorable [6]'); it is not stated whether respondents saw them or they are coding only. Suggested: Confirm with the authors. (p. 19, p. 20)
