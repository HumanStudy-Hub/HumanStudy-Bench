# Experiment 2: Single and multiple utterances

`exp2`

Preregistered 2x2 between-subjects replication (antonym type x context) with adjective sets that have both morphological and lexical antonyms.

## Hypotheses

### H1: Partial vs. full ordering by antonym type

`h-order`

- **Statement**: In isolation (single utterance), unhappy (morphological antonym) is not distinguished from not happy (partial ordering), whereas lexical antonyms (short, sad) are interpreted more negatively than negated positives (full ordering). (p. 3, p. 4)
- **Scope**: Interpretations of adjectives describing properties of people, rated on a slider between the most positive and most negative person. (p. 4)

### H2: Context modulates morphological antonyms

`h-context`

- **Statement** (verbatim, p. 6, p. 3):
  > Morphological antonyms will be interpreted more negatively than negated positives in a context with multiple utterances.
- **Scope**: Morphological antonym sets; multiple-utterance vs. single-utterance context. (p. 6)

## Procedure

Order of steps:

- Single-utterance session (p. 5)
  - conditions: Morphological antonyms, single utterance; Lexical antonyms, single utterance
  - Repeated: 12 single-sentence trials (p. 5)
    - conditions: Morphological antonyms, single utterance; Lexical antonyms, single utterance
    - **Repeat rule**: 12 trials, exactly 3 repetitions of each adjective type, each from a unique adjective set. (p. 5)
    - Read one sentence and rate the person (participant sees: Exp 2 adjective sets (Table 2); Exp 2 response scale) (p. 5)
      - conditions: Morphological antonyms, single utterance; Lexical antonyms, single utterance
- Multiple-utterance session (p. 5)
  - conditions: Morphological antonyms, multiple utterances; Lexical antonyms, multiple utterances
  - Repeated: Displays of four statements (12 ratings in total) (p. 5)
    - conditions: Morphological antonyms, multiple utterances; Lexical antonyms, multiple utterances
    - **Repeat rule [derived, to check]**: All four adjective types rated simultaneously, each for a different person, for a total of 12 trials; grouping of the 12 trials into displays is assumed (3 displays of four). (p. 5)
      - how it was derived: Assumes 12 trials = 12 ratings shown four at a time.
    - Rate four statements shown together (participant sees: Exp 2 adjective sets (Table 2); Exp 2 response scale) (p. 5)
      - conditions: Morphological antonyms, multiple utterances; Lexical antonyms, multiple utterances

## Analysis

### Exp 2 single-utterance mixed model and simple effects

`an2-single`

- **Aim**: Test whether the antonym vs. negated-positive contrast differs between morphological and lexical antonyms in single-utterance conditions. (p. 5, p. 6)
- **Inputs**: Unnormalized ratings from the single-utterance conditions only. (p. 5)
- **Method**: Linear mixed model with antonym type, adjective type (Helmert) and interaction; random intercepts and random slopes of adjective type by participant and by item; then simple-effects models (random slope by item dropped for lexical antonyms to converge). (p. 6)
- **Preregistration**: Exclusion criterion, sample size, procedure and analysis preregistered. (p. 5)

### Exp 2 context model (morphological antonyms)

`an2-ctx`

- **Aim**: Test whether context modulates the antonym vs. negated-positive difference for morphological antonyms. (p. 6)
- **Inputs**: Raw ratings from morphological antonym conditions (single and multiple). (p. 6)
- **Method**: Linear mixed model with adjective type, context and their interaction; random intercepts and random slopes of adjective type by participant and by item. (p. 6)

### Exp 2 exploratory three-way model

`an2-3way`

- **Aim**: Exploratory: antonym vs. negated positive x antonym type x context interaction in a full model. (p. 6)
- **Method [to check]**: Full three-way interactive mixed model (exploratory); structure not further described. (p. 6)

## Simulation scope

### Exp 2 simulation scope

`sim2`

- **Steps [implementation]**: Every rating trial can be replayed as a text prompt: statement plus slider description, one AI participant per original participant. (p. 5)
- **Roles [implementation]**: All original participants are AI participants; no scripted roles exist in the paper. (p. 5)
- **Conditions [implementation]**: All four between-subjects arms run; single-utterance arms prompt trial by trial, multiple-utterance arms show four statements together. (p. 5)
- **Outcomes [implementation]**: Slider ratings are collected as numbers; the paper's linear mixed models are approximated offline (see evaluation), since lme4 is not available. (p. 5)
- **Limits [implementation]**: Slider is replaced by a typed number on an assumed 0-100 scale; instruction and sentence wording are replay adaptations; MTurk population, attention and the native-language screen cannot be reproduced; AI agreement does not show human behavior. (p. 5)
