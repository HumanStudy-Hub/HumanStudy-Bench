# Post hoc comparison with Many Labs 2 (analysis only)

`s2`

Reanalysis comparing this project's main-effect estimates with those of Many Labs 2 (Klein et al., 2018), using both projects' subject-level data; not an experiment.

## Analysis

### Comparison of main effect with Many Labs 2

`a_ml2`

- **Aim**: Explore why Many Labs 2 found a main effect while this project did not. (p. 10)
- **Method**: Compared U.S. MTurk samples; reanalysed raw Many Labs 2 data with LMM, GEE and raw mean differences; redefined sampling frames; pooled main effect across primary-analysis sites. (p. 10, p. 21)
- **Inputs**: Subject-level data of Many Labs 5 and Many Labs 2 (primary-analysis sites; load-condition subjects of this project excluded). (p. 10, p. 21)
- **Protocol differences**: Likelihood scale 0–10 (11 options) in Many Labs 5 vs 1–10 in Many Labs 2; skipping allowed in Many Labs 2; Many Labs 2 ran it within a 30-minute randomized block. (p. 20)

## Reported results

### Many Labs 2 comparison results

`r_ml2`

- **result_kind**: reported (p. 21)
- **Difference**: Many Labs 5 main effect smaller than Many Labs 2: LMM −0.44 [−0.92, 0.04], p = .07; discrepancy remained unexplained; pooled estimate showed a small main effect. (p. 21, p. 10)
- **Many Labs 2 as cited**: Main effect strongly supported in Many Labs 2 but smaller than in the original. (p. 3)
