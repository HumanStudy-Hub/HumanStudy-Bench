# Open issues

What the paper does not state or what still needs a decision.

- **Headline pictures and lede sentences are not in the paper** [decision, `iss_s1_lede`, m_headlines]: Headlines were shown as Facebook posts with a picture and lede sentence; the paper and supplement print only the headline text. Suggested: Supply the OSF materials, or confirm that a text-only headline is an acceptable replay adaptation. (p. 3)
- **Survey introduction and instructions are not printed** [decision, `iss_instructions`, m_instructions]: The paper does not reproduce the consent text or task instructions. Suggested: Provide the original instruction text from OSF to replace the replay frame. (p. 3)
- **Individual-differences item wording is not printed** [check, `iss_indiff_items`, m_indiff_items]: CRT, science quiz, MMS and demographic items are described but not reproduced. Suggested: Supply the OSF item wording if the correlates should be replayed. (p. 3)
- **Screener question text is not printed** [check, `iss_screener_text`, m_screeners]: Only one example is described; the full text is on OSF. Suggested: Supply the screener text if attentiveness should be replayed. (p. 4)
- **Number of participants per condition is not reported** [check, `iss_s1_percond`, p_s1]: The paper gives the total (853) only. Suggested: Take the per-condition n from the OSF data if needed. (p. 3)
- **Participant compensation is not reported** [check, `iss_s1_comp`, p_s1]: The method does not mention payment. Suggested: Check the OSF preregistration. (p. 3)
- **Table 1 prints 0.236*** (0.0207***) for the MMS sharing/false cell** [check, `iss_s1_t1_print`, res_s1_t1]: The controlled value 0.0207 is much smaller than every neighbouring value and looks like a typographical slip (possibly 0.207). Suggested: Check the OSF output. (p. 5)
- **Order of the individual-differences measures relative to the news task is not stated** [check, `iss_s1_order`, study1]: The paper only says the COVID-19 questions came before the news task and that screeners were placed just before it, after the CRT and after the science quiz/MMS. Suggested: Take the order from the OSF survey file. (p. 4)
- **How the pretest headline was chosen is not stated** [check, `iss_s2_pretest_assign`, m_s2_pretest]: The paper says each participant saw one of four possible headlines. Suggested: Take the rule from OSF. (p. 6)
- **Number of participants per condition is not reported** [check, `iss_s2_percond`, p_s2]: The paper gives the total (856) only. Suggested: Take it from the OSF data. (p. 6)
- **Participant compensation is not reported** [check, `iss_s2_comp`, p_s2]: The method does not mention payment. Suggested: Check the OSF preregistration. (p. 6)
- **Order of the additional measures relative to the sharing task is not stated** [check, `iss_s2_order`, study2]: The paper only says the additional measures of Study 1 were included. Suggested: Take the order from the OSF survey file. (p. 7)
