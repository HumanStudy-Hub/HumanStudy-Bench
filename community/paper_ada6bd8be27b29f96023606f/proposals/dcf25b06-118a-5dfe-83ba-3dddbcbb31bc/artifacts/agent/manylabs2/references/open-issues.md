# Open issues

What the paper does not state or what still needs a decision.

- **Participants per slate not reported (Slate 1)** [check, `iss-n-slate1`, p-slate1]: The paper reports 64 samples for the slate and a total of 15,305 participants, but not the participant count of the slate. Suggested: Take the count from the public dataset (https://osf.io/8cd4r/) if wanted. (p. 15)
- **Compensation not reported (Slate 1)** [check, `iss-comp-slate1`, p-slate1]: The paper does not say how participants were compensated; sites decided locally. Suggested: Record per-site compensation if the researcher needs it.
- **Assignment to conditions not described (Slate 1)** [decision, `iss-assign-slate1`, d-slate1]: The paper states random assignment of labs to slates and random task order, but not the rule that assigned participants to the versions of each effect. Suggested: Confirm random assignment per effect with participant-level randomization. (p. 16)

- **Picture stimulus in a text replay: Direction and socioeconomic status** [decision, `iss-modal-huan`, m-huan-high]: The response is a click on the map picture, so a participant must be able to see the picture and reply with a position; the codebook gives no reply format for it. The picture is supplied in the codebook, but the AI participants launched by the replay runner receive text only. Suggested: Decide whether to run this effect with a vision-capable participant (the package passes the picture files to such a participant) or to exclude it. (p. 23)
- **Visual manipulation in a text replay: Disfluency engages analytical processing** [decision, `iss-modal-alt`, m-alt-easy]: The disfluency manipulation is a visual font property that a text-only AI participant cannot perceive. Suggested: Decide whether to run this effect with a vision-capable participant, or exclude it. (p. 26)
- **Which version is which condition: Disfluency engages analytical processing** [check, `iss-map-alt`, c-alt-easy]: Codebook alt1 and alt2 carry identical text; the paper states the two fonts but not which codebook version uses which. Suggested: Confirm the mapping against the OSF Qualtrics file. (p. 26)
- **Which version is which condition: Trolley dilemma 1** [check, `iss-map-haus12`, c-haus12-side]: The paper does not say which codebook version is which; haus1 is the passenger (Denise) scenario. Suggested: Confirm the mapping against the OSF Qualtrics file. (p. 37)
