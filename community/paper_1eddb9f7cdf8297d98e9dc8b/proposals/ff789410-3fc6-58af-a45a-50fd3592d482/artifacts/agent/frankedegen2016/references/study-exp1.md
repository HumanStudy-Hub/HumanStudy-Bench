# Experiment 1: comprehension in reference games

`exp1`

Mechanical Turk participants choose the intended referent among three objects after seeing a pictorial one-feature message; simple and complex ad hoc Quantity implicature trials plus fillers.

## Participants

### Participants (AI participants replace every original participant)

`e1_part`

- **Setting [to check]**: Online, Amazon Mechanical Turk. (p. 7)
- **Recruitment [to check]**: Recruited via Mechanical Turk; IP address limited to US addresses; past work approval rate of at least 95%. (p. 7)
- **N recruited [to check]**: 60 (p. 7)
- **N analysed [to check]**: 51 (p. 8)
- **N completed**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Exclusions [to check]**: The 15% of participants with the highest error rate on trials that were not ambiguous were excluded (a minimum error rate of 5%; one of them was not a self-reported native English speaker). (p. 8)
- **Compensation [to check]**: $1.00 per participant (hourly rate of $10.00); written consent; Stanford University research subjects review board approval. (p. 7)
- **Sampling unit [derived, to check]**: One participant sees all trial types (within-participant). (p. 7)
  - how it was derived: Every participant saw 66 trials spanning all trial types.
- **Characteristics**: Not stated in the paper.

## Design

### Design

`e1_design`

- **Type [to check]**: Within-participant. Critical implicature trials (12 simple, 12 complex) and filler trials (ambiguous and unambiguous baselines and others) are intermixed in one sequence. (p. 7, p. 8)
- **Order [to check]**: Trial order randomized, as were target, competitor and distractor order. (p. 8)
- **Baselines [to check]**: Unambiguous fillers set the upper bound on target choices; ambiguous fillers show how often the target is chosen by chance. (p. 8)
- **Assignment [derived, to check]**: No between-participant conditions are reported. (p. 7)
  - how it was derived: All trial types are within the single participant group.
- **Blinding**: Not stated in the paper.

## Conditions

### Simple implicature trials (12)

`e1_c_simple`

- **Definition [to check]**: Target is the inexpressible-feature object (robot with hat or monster with scarf) and the competitor shares the message feature. (p. 8)

### Complex implicature trials (12)

`e1_c_complex`

- **Definition [to check]**: Competitor and target share the message feature and differ in an expressible feature (two-step implicature). (p. 8)

### Unambiguous filler trials

`e1_c_unamb`

- **Definition [to check]**: Only one object has the trigger message feature (p. 8)

### Ambiguous filler trials

`e1_c_amb`

- **Definition [to check]**: Two identical objects are equally viable target candidates (p. 8)

## Materials

### Stimuli and instructions

`e1_mat`

- **Stimulus structure [to check]**: Each display shows three objects crossing kind (robot, green monster, purple monster) with accessory (scarf, blue hat, red hat); four expressible features (green monster, purple monster, red hat, blue hat) appear as pictorial messages at the bottom of every display; robot and scarf are inexpressible. (p. 7)
- **Trial counts [to check]**: 66 trials: 24 critical (12 simple, 12 complex) and 42 fillers (24 on implicature displays, 18 unambiguous/ambiguous baselines). (p. 7, p. 8)
- **Printed instructions (verbatim)**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stimulus images (verbatim)**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stimulus generation rules [to check]**: See evidence: the target, competitor and distractor are generated by feature constraints per condition after randomly sampling a message; filler displays are re-used implicature displays with a different target plus baseline displays. (p. 8)

## Procedure

Order of steps:

- Four speaker trials (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 7)
- Repeated: 66 comprehension trials in random order (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 7, p. 8)
  - Simple implicature trial (x12) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 8)
    - conditions: Simple implicature trials (12)
  - Complex implicature trial (x12) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 8)
    - conditions: Complex implicature trials (12)
  - Filler trial on an implicature display (x24) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 8)
  - Unambiguous or ambiguous baseline filler (x18) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 8)
    - conditions: Unambiguous filler trials; Ambiguous filler trials

### Procedure

`e1_proc`

- **Task [to check]**: Referential comprehension: pick which of three objects a pictorial one-feature message refers to. (p. 7)
- **Actions [to check]**: Four speaker trials first, then 66 comprehension trials. (p. 7)
- **What is visible [to check]**: Three objects, the received message and the four expressible features at the bottom of the display on every trial. (p. 7)
- **Feedback**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stopping [derived, to check]**: After 66 trials. (p. 7)
  - how it was derived: Each participant saw 66 experimental trials.
- **Source of messages**: Not stated in the paper.

## Variables

### Choice (dependent variable)

`e1_v_choice`

- **Role [derived, to check]**: dv (p. 9)
  - how it was derived: Target versus competitor choice is the outcome of the regression.
- **Type [to check]**: Categorical per trial: target, competitor or distractor object; binary (target vs competitor) in the regression after excluding distractor choices. (p. 9)
- **Observation unit [derived, to check]**: One trial of one participant. (p. 7)
  - how it was derived: Each participant saw 66 trials and choices are counted per trial.
- **Definition [to check]**: The object the participant picks given the pictorial message. (p. 7)

### Trial condition (independent variable)

`e1_v_cond`

- **Role [derived, to check]**: iv (p. 9)
  - how it was derived: Helmert-coded CONDITION is the predictor of interest.
- **Type [to check]**: Categorical with levels simple, complex, ambiguous filler (unambiguous fillers excluded from the regression for lack of variance) (p. 9)
- **Observation unit [derived, to check]**: One trial. (p. 7)
  - how it was derived: Condition is assigned per trial.

### Control predictors

`e1_v_cov`

- **Role [to check]**: covariate (p. 9)
- **Definition [to check]**: Centered trial number and its interactions with the Helmert contrasts, message type (accessory vs. species, centered) and target position (left as reference level). (p. 9)

## Analysis

### Mixed-effects regression

`e1_an`

- **Aim [to check]**: Test whether target choices differ between simple and complex implicature situations and between complex implicature and ambiguous filler trials, and whether there are learning effects. (p. 9)
- **Method [to check]**: Logistic mixed-effects regression of target versus competitor choices; Helmert-coded CONDITION (harder vs. simple; ambiguous vs. complex), centered trial number and its interactions, message type, target position; random by-participant intercepts and slopes for message type and trial number. (p. 9)
- **Inputs [to check]**: Choices on simple, complex and ambiguous filler trials; trials with distractor choices (1% in Experiment 1) excluded. (p. 9)
- **Criterion [to check]**: Reported p-values for coefficients (Table 2). (p. 10)
- **Participant exclusion [to check]**: Exclude the 15% of participants with the highest error rate on non-ambiguous trials. (p. 8)

## Reported results

### Choice proportions

`e1_r_desc`

- **Result kind**: reported (p. 8, p. 9)
- **Descriptives [to check]**: Unambiguous fillers: 99% target vs .04% competitor; ambiguous fillers: 46% target vs 51% competitor; simple: 77% target, 23% competitor; complex: 57% target, 42% competitor (Fig 2 left). (p. 8, p. 9)

### Regression

`e1_r_reg`

- **Result kind**: reported (p. 9, p. 10)
- **Effects [to check]**: Simple > complex: beta = 1.28, SE = .12, p < .0001. Complex > ambiguous: beta = .44, SE = .13, p < .001. Trial number and its interactions n.s.; centre vs left target position beta = 1.28 (SE .14), right vs left .74 (SE .13), both p < .0001; message type n.s. (p. 9, p. 10)
- **Individual variability [to check]**: Individual target-choice proportions are not mirrored by the aggregate pattern; there is substantial individual variability (Fig 2 right). (p. 10)

## Simulation scope

### Simulation scope

`e1_sim`

- **Steps [implementation, to check]**: All 66 trials (preceded by four speaker trials in Experiment 1) can be presented one at a time as text; each trial prompt includes the participant's own earlier trials and choices. (p. 7)
- **Roles [implementation, to check]**: Each original participant is one AI participant. Messages are generated stimuli (scripted by the design) in comprehension; no addressee in production. (p. 7)
- **Conditions [implementation, to check]**: All four trial types plus the filler classes are within-participant. (p. 7)
- **Outcomes [implementation, to check]**: Target, competitor and distractor choice per trial; per-condition proportions and the Helmert contrasts are computed; mixed-effects estimates are approximated by fixed-effects logistic regression. (p. 9)
- **Limits [implementation, to check]**: Pictures are rendered as text, instruction wording is paraphrased, MTurk context, payment and pace cannot be simulated, and AI participants are not a model of human samples. (p. 7)

## Open issues

- **exp1: sample characteristics and completion counts not reported** [check, `is_e1_part`, e1_part]: The paper gives no age, gender or the number who completed versus started. Suggested: Treat participants as unconditioned AI participants or supply demographics.
- **exp1: number completed not stated** [check, `is_e1_part2`, e1_part]: Only the recruited (60) and analysed counts are reported. Suggested: Supply the completion count.
- **exp1: blinding not reported** [check, `is_e1_blind`, e1_design]: The paper does not state blinding. Suggested: None needed unless the researcher wants a statement.
- **exp1: printed instructions and stimuli not in the paper** [decision, `is_e1_instr`, e1_mat]: The paper paraphrases what participants were told but prints no instruction text, screen or picture. Suggested: Supply the original instruction text and stimulus pictures in sources/ to make them verbatim.
- **exp1: stimuli are pictures** [check, `is_e1_img`, e1_mat]: The original stimuli are pictures (Fig 1) which text-only AI participants cannot see. Suggested: Accept the text rendering or provide image-capable participants.
- **exp1: filler displays under-specified** [decision, `is_e1_fill`, e1_mat]: The 18 baseline fillers are described only as unambiguous or ambiguous; the split between the two kinds, the number of distinct displays and scoring details are not stated. Suggested: Supply the split and the filler construction or confirm the assumption. (p. 8)
- **exp1: feedback not reported** [check, `is_e1_fb`, e1_proc]: The paper does not say whether participants received feedback on their choices. Suggested: Confirm that no feedback was given.
- **exp1: counterpart of the participant not specified** [check, `is_e1_snd`, e1_proc]: It is not stated whether the messages (comprehension) or the addressee (production) were real participants or scripted. Suggested: Confirm that messages were generated from the design, not taken from earlier participants.
- **exp1: Helmert contrast scaling not stated** [check, `is_e1_helmert`, e1_an]: The contrast weights are not printed, so coefficients are only comparable with the paper up to the coding. Suggested: Confirm weights or run glmer externally.
