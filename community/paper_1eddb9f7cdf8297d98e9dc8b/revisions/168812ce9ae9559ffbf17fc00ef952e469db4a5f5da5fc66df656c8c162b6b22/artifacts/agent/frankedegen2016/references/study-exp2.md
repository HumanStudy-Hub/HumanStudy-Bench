# Experiment 2: production in reference games

`exp2`

Mechanical Turk participants choose one of four pictorial messages to describe a highlighted object in the same games.

## Participants

### Participants (AI participants replace every original participant)

`e2_part`

- **Setting [to check]**: Online, Amazon Mechanical Turk. (p. 10)
- **Recruitment [to check]**: Recruited via Mechanical Turk; IP address limited to US addresses; past work approval rate of at least 95%. (p. 10)
- **N recruited [to check]**: 60 (p. 10)
- **N analysed [to check]**: 50 (p. 12)
- **N completed**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Exclusions [to check]**: The 15% of participants with the highest error rate on trials that were not ambiguous were excluded (a minimum error rate of 10%; two of them were not self-reported native speakers of English). (p. 11, p. 12)
- **Compensation [to check]**: $1.00 per participant (hourly rate of $10.00); written consent; Stanford University research subjects review board approval. (p. 7)
- **Sampling unit [derived, to check]**: One participant sees all trial types (within-participant). (p. 11)
  - how it was derived: Every participant saw 66 trials spanning all trial types.
- **Characteristics**: Not stated in the paper.

## Design

### Design

`e2_design`

- **Type [to check]**: Within-participant. Critical implicature trials (12 simple, 12 complex) and filler trials (ambiguous and unambiguous baselines and others) are intermixed in one sequence. (p. 11)
- **Order [to check]**: Trial order randomized, with trigger position and message order randomized. (p. 11)
- **Baselines [to check]**: Unambiguous fillers set the upper bound on target choices; ambiguous fillers show how often the target is chosen by chance. (p. 11)
- **Assignment [derived, to check]**: No between-participant conditions are reported. (p. 11)
  - how it was derived: All trial types are within the single participant group.
- **Blinding**: Not stated in the paper.

## Conditions

### Simple implicature trials (12)

`e2_c_simple`

- **Definition [to check]**: Target message is more informative than the competitor message; second object is the robot/scarf object sharing the competitor feature. (p. 11)

### Complex implicature trials (12)

`e2_c_complex`

- **Definition [to check]**: Competitor and target share the message feature and differ in an expressible feature (two-step implicature). (p. 11)

### Unambiguous filler trials

`e2_c_unamb`

- **Definition [to check]**: The highlighted object has one expressible and one inexpressible feature, so the target message is the only good choice. (p. 11)

### Ambiguous filler trials

`e2_c_amb`

- **Definition [to check]**: The highlighted object has two expressible features that no other object shares; each is an equally good message. (p. 11)

## Materials

### Stimuli and instructions

`e2_mat`

- **Stimulus structure [to check]**: Each display shows three objects crossing kind (robot, green monster, purple monster) with accessory (scarf, blue hat, red hat); four expressible features (green monster, purple monster, red hat, blue hat) appear as pictorial messages at the bottom of every display; robot and scarf are inexpressible. (p. 7)
- **Trial counts [to check]**: 66 trials: 24 critical (12 simple, 12 complex) and 42 fillers (24 on implicature displays, 18 unambiguous/ambiguous baselines). (p. 11)
- **Printed instructions (verbatim)**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stimulus images (verbatim)**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stimulus generation rules [to check]**: See evidence: the target, competitor and distractor are generated by feature constraints per condition after randomly sampling a message; filler displays are re-used implicature displays with a different target plus baseline displays. (p. 11)

## Procedure

Order of steps:

- Repeated: 66 production trials in random order (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 11)
  - Simple implicature trial (x12) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 11)
    - conditions: Simple implicature trials (12)
  - Complex implicature trial (x12) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 11)
    - conditions: Complex implicature trials (12)
  - Filler trial on an implicature display (x24) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 11)
  - Unambiguous or ambiguous baseline filler (x18) (participant sees: Stimuli and instructions) (p. 11)
    - conditions: Unambiguous filler trials; Ambiguous filler trials

### Procedure

`e2_proc`

- **Task [to check]**: Reference production: click one of four pictorial messages to get another worker to pick the highlighted object. (p. 11)
- **Actions [to check]**: 66 production trials. (p. 11)
- **What is visible [to check]**: Three objects with the trigger object highlighted by a yellow rectangle and the four pictorial messages. (p. 11, p. 7)
- **Feedback**: Not stated in the paper.
- **Stopping [derived, to check]**: After 66 trials. (p. 11)
  - how it was derived: Each participant saw 66 experimental trials.
- **Addressee**: Not stated in the paper.

## Variables

### Choice (dependent variable)

`e2_v_choice`

- **Role [derived, to check]**: dv (p. 12)
  - how it was derived: Target versus competitor choice is the outcome of the regression.
- **Type [to check]**: Categorical per trial: target, competitor or distractor message; binary (target vs competitor) in the regression after excluding distractor choices. (p. 12)
- **Observation unit [derived, to check]**: One trial of one participant. (p. 11)
  - how it was derived: Each participant saw 66 trials and choices are counted per trial.
- **Definition [to check]**: The pictorial message the participant picks to describe the highlighted object. (p. 11)

### Trial condition (independent variable)

`e2_v_cond`

- **Role [derived, to check]**: iv (p. 12)
  - how it was derived: Helmert-coded CONDITION is the predictor of interest.
- **Type [to check]**: Categorical: simple, complex, ambiguous (unambiguous fillers excluded from regression). (p. 12)
- **Observation unit [derived, to check]**: One trial. (p. 11)
  - how it was derived: Condition is assigned per trial.

### Control predictors

`e2_v_cov`

- **Role [to check]**: covariate (p. 12)
- **Definition [to check]**: Trial number (and interactions with the contrasts), message type, and target position centered numeric 1 to 4. (p. 12)

## Analysis

### Mixed-effects regression

`e2_an`

- **Aim [to check]**: Test whether target choices differ between simple and complex implicature situations and between complex implicature and ambiguous filler trials, and whether there are learning effects. (p. 12)
- **Method [to check]**: As Experiment 1, but with random by-participant intercepts only and target position coded as a centered numeric position (1 to 4). (p. 12)
- **Inputs [to check]**: Choices on simple, complex and ambiguous filler trials of retained participants. (p. 12)
- **Criterion [to check]**: Reported p-values for coefficients (Table 2). (p. 12)
- **Participant exclusion [to check]**: Exclude the 15% of participants with the highest error rate on non-ambiguous trials. (p. 11)

## Reported results

### Choice proportions

`e2_r_desc`

- **Result kind**: reported (p. 12)
- **Descriptives [to check]**: Unambiguous fillers: 96% target vs 1% competitor; ambiguous fillers: 48% vs 48%; simple: 82% target, 16% competitor; complex: 53% target, 45% competitor (Fig 3 left). (p. 12)

### Regression

`e2_r_reg`

- **Result kind**: reported (p. 12)
- **Effects [to check]**: Simple > complex: beta = 1.63, SE = .13, p < .0001. Complex > ambiguous: beta = .2, SE = .11, p < .08 (marginal). Trial number beta = -.01, SE = .003, p < .05; message type beta = .41, SE = .10, p < .0001 (more target choices for species messages). (p. 12)
- **Individual variability [to check]**: Individual target-choice proportions are not mirrored by the aggregate pattern; there is substantial individual variability (Fig 3 right). (p. 13)

## Simulation scope

### Simulation scope

`e2_sim`

- **Steps [implementation, to check]**: All 66 trials (preceded by four speaker trials in Experiment 1) can be presented one at a time as text; each trial prompt includes the participant's own earlier trials and choices. (p. 11)
- **Roles [implementation, to check]**: Each original participant is one AI participant. Messages are generated stimuli (scripted by the design) in comprehension; no addressee in production. (p. 11)
- **Conditions [implementation, to check]**: All four trial types plus the filler classes are within-participant. (p. 11)
- **Outcomes [implementation, to check]**: Target, competitor and distractor choice per trial; per-condition proportions and the Helmert contrasts are computed; mixed-effects estimates are approximated by fixed-effects logistic regression. (p. 12)
- **Limits [implementation, to check]**: Pictures are rendered as text, instruction wording is paraphrased, MTurk context, payment and pace cannot be simulated, and AI participants are not a model of human samples. (p. 11)

## Open issues

- **exp2: sample characteristics and completion counts not reported** [check, `is_e2_part`, e2_part]: The paper gives no age, gender or the number who completed versus started. Suggested: Treat participants as unconditioned AI participants or supply demographics.
- **exp2: number completed not stated** [check, `is_e2_part2`, e2_part]: Only the recruited (60) and analysed counts are reported. Suggested: Supply the completion count.
- **exp2: blinding not reported** [check, `is_e2_blind`, e2_design]: The paper does not state blinding. Suggested: None needed unless the researcher wants a statement.
- **exp2: printed instructions and stimuli not in the paper** [decision, `is_e2_instr`, e2_mat]: The paper paraphrases what participants were told but prints no instruction text, screen or picture. Suggested: Supply the original instruction text and stimulus pictures in sources/ to make them verbatim.
- **exp2: stimuli are pictures** [check, `is_e2_img`, e2_mat]: The original stimuli are pictures (Fig 1) which text-only AI participants cannot see. Suggested: Accept the text rendering or provide image-capable participants.
- **exp2: filler displays under-specified** [decision, `is_e2_fill`, e2_mat]: The 18 baseline fillers are described only as unambiguous or ambiguous; the split between the two kinds, the number of distinct displays and scoring details are not stated, and which object counts as 'competitor from the simple condition' for production fillers is not defined. Suggested: Supply the split and the filler construction or confirm the assumption. (p. 11)
- **exp2: feedback not reported** [check, `is_e2_fb`, e2_proc]: The paper does not say whether participants received feedback on their choices. Suggested: Confirm that no feedback was given.
- **exp2: counterpart of the participant not specified** [check, `is_e2_snd`, e2_proc]: It is not stated whether the messages (comprehension) or the addressee (production) were real participants or scripted. Suggested: Confirm that messages were generated from the design, not taken from earlier participants.
- **exp2: Helmert contrast scaling not stated** [check, `is_e2_helmert`, e2_an]: The contrast weights are not printed, so coefficients are only comparable with the paper up to the coding. Suggested: Confirm weights or run glmer externally.
