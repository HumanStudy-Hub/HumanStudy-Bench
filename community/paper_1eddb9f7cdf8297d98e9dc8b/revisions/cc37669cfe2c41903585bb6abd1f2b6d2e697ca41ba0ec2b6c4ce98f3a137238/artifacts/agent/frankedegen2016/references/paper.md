# Paper context

## Overview

`ov`

- **Year [to check]**: 2016 (p. 1)
- **Method type [to check]**: Two online behavioural experiments (reference-game comprehension and production) analysed with a hierarchical Bayesian latent-type model; a third experiment eliciting salience priors is reported only in a supplement (S4 Text). (p. 7)
- **Summary [to check]**: The paper asks whether speakers and listeners are Gricean individually or only on average, using reference games that need ad hoc Quantity implicatures of varying complexity. A heterogeneous mix of reasoning types predicts the data better than a homogeneous Gricean population, especially for comprehension. (p. 1)

## Question and gap

`bg`

- **Research question [to check]**: Are listeners and speakers Gricean at the individual level or only at the population level? (p. 21, p. 1)
- **Gap [to check]**: Probabilistic pragmatics models are usually assessed on population-level data obtained by averaging over participants, which implicitly assumes a homogeneous population. (p. 2)
- **Theory [to check]**: Rational Speech Act (Gricean S1 speaker, R2 listener) versus iterated best response types S0, S1, S2 and R0, R1, R2 with increasing Theory-of-Mind depth. (p. 4, p. 5)

## Validity and scope

`lim`

- **Validity statements [to check]**: Results are contingent on the particular task, the data and the specific modelling choices; reference-game data do not necessarily generalise to other areas of language use. (p. 19, p. 3)
- **Modelling limitations [to check]**: Population-level lambda and epsilon are shared across reasoning types and participants; reasoning type is assumed constant over the experiment, which the authors call generally dubious. (p. 19, p. 15)
- **Replications the paper reports [to check]**: Experiment 1 is described as a replication of Experiment 1 of reference [9]; Experiment 2 as a replication of Experiment 2 of [9], with caveats (target choices slightly above chance in the complex condition, below ceiling in the simple condition). (p. 10, p. 13)
- **Materials [open decision]**: Links to all experiments are given in S3 Text and the derivations in S1, S2, S4 and S5 Text; these supplements are not in paper.pdf. (p. 7, p. 18)
