# Study 2: all E2s in Part 2, valence rated in Part 3

`study2`

Three parts. Procedure as Study 1 except that E2 for all events is collected in Part 2 and valence ratings in Part 3. N = 100 (Prolific, UK).

## Hypotheses

### Central hypothesis

`n-hyp`

- **Statement [to check]**: Participants will update asymmetrically in response to neutral information. (p. 9)
- **Scope [to check]**: Pre-registered; no prediction of differences among the three procedural variants was made. (p. 5, p. 3)

`p-s2`

- **Roles [to check]**: All participants take every part of the task; no scripted roles or confederates. (p. 6)
- **Recruitment [to check]**: Prolific Academic, pool restricted to the United Kingdom because the stimuli were compiled for this population; samples of the three studies independent. (p. 6)
- **N recruited [to check]**: 100 (p. 5)

## Design

### Design (shared by Studies 1-3)

`n-design`

- **Design type [to check]**: 3 (event valence: negative, neutral, positive) x 2 (direction of error: upwards, downwards) quasi-experimental within-subjects design, run in Qualtrics. Valence and direction are coded trial by trial from participants' own responses, not assigned. (p. 6)
- **Assignment [to check]**: Order of the two initial estimates (E1 then eBR, or eBR then E1) randomly assigned between subjects. (p. 6)
- **Order [to check]**: Events presented in randomized order in each part. (p. 7)
- **Focal comparison [to check]**: Main analysis restricted to trials the participant rated 3 ('neither positive nor negative'); event valence is a post-treatment variable (rated after the BR in Studies 1-2, before E2 in Study 3). (p. 6, p. 11, p. 7)
- **Blinding**: Not stated in the paper.

`cond-s2-e1first`

- **Assignment [derived, to check]**: Random assignment between subjects. The paper states the procedure of this study follows Study 1 except the stated change, so the same counterbalancing is taken to apply (see issue). (p. 6, p. 7)

## Materials

- **Sources [to check]**: 2 events from Shah et al. (2016); 21 from Garrett and Sharot (2017) (rated neutral by pilot participants, 3.00 ± 0.99); 28 novel events with base rates gathered or calculated from external sources (e.g., Ofcom, BBC). (p. 6)

## Procedure

Order of steps:

- Repeated: Part 1: 51 events in randomized order (estimates, BR, recall) (p. 6, p. 7)
  - Estimate E1, then eBR (participant sees: Stimuli: 51 life events with base rates) (p. 6, p. 7)
    - conditions: Study 2: E1 then eBR
  - See the actual base rate (BR) (participant sees: Stimuli: 51 life events with base rates; BR: presented base rate) (p. 6, p. 7)
  - Write down the BR (BR Recall); corrected if wrong (participant sees: BR: presented base rate) (p. 6, p. 7)
  - Estimate eBR, then E1 (participant sees: Stimuli: 51 life events with base rates) (p. 6, p. 7)
    - conditions: Study 2: eBR then E1
  - (back to: See the actual base rate (BR))
  - (back to: See the actual base rate (BR))
  - (back to: Write down the BR (BR Recall); corrected if wrong)
- Repeated: Part 2: E2 for all events (p. 7)
  - Re-estimate E2 (participant sees: Stimuli: 51 life events with base rates) (p. 6, p. 7)
- Repeated: Part 3: valence rating of all events (p. 7)
  - Rate the valence of the event (participant sees: Stimuli: 51 life events with base rates) (p. 7)

## Analysis

### Exclusion criteria (all studies)

`a-exclusion`

- **Aim [to check]**: Remove outlying updates and uncodable trials before analysis. (p. 7)
- **Criterion [to check]**: Mean updates in each of the six valence x direction cells were calculated and outliers removed (±3 × the interquartile range, per the paper's footnote); trials with E1 equal to BR excluded. (p. 7)
- **Trials excluded [to check]**: From 5,100 trials per study: 339 (6.65%), 414 (8.12%), 431 (8.45%) excluded in Studies 1, 2, 3. (p. 7)
- **Robustness [to check]**: All results hold without the exclusion criterion except one supplementary result (regression analysis, Study 1). (p. 12)

### Main analysis: LMM on neutral trials

`a-main`

- **Aim [to check]**: Test whether update value differs between upwards and downwards direction of error on trials rated neutral. (p. 9)
- **Inputs [to check]**: Neutral-rated trials after exclusions: update value, direction of error, participant. (p. 9)
- **Method [to check]**: Linear mixed effects model: update value as DV, direction of error as fixed factor, participant as random factor (random intercepts only after reducing a maximal model); Type III tests, Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. (p. 9)
- **Robustness [to check]**: Maximal-model statistics are in SI Table S2 (not supplied); results said to hold across specifications. (p. 9)

### Secondary analysis: valence x direction LMM

`a-secondary`

- **Aim [to check]**: Compare the asymmetry on neutral trials with that on negative and positive trials. (p. 10)
- **Method [to check]**: LMM with event valence and direction of error and their interaction; random slopes and intercepts by participant for direction of error, no correlation parameters (maximal model in SI Table S3, not supplied). (p. 10)
- **Caveat [to check]**: Conditioned on a post-treatment variable (valence rated after BR), so post-treatment bias is possible. (p. 11)

## Reported results

### Study 2: LMM on neutral trials

`r-s2-main`

- **Result kind [to check]**: reported (p. 10)
- **Descriptives [to check]**: 1,699 neutral trials: 831 upwards (M = 4.16, SD = 10.01), 868 downwards (M = 10.08, SD = 18.06). (p. 10)
- **Test [to check]**: F(1,1694) = 77.09, p < 0.001. (p. 10)
- **Effect [to check]**: Upwards direction of error decreased update by about 6.24 percentage points (SE 0.71). (p. 10)
- **Excluded trials [to check]**: 414 of 5,100 (8.12%). (p. 7)

### Study 2: valence x direction LMM

`r-s2-sec`

- **Result kind [to check]**: reported (p. 11)
- **Test [to check]**: Direction of error F(1,98) = 112.85; event valence F(2,4656) = 35.49; interaction F(2,4610) = 43.86; all p < 0.001. (p. 11)
- **Descriptives [to check]**: Negative (1,582 trials): downwards n = 692, M = 13.08, SD = 21.33; upwards n = 890, M = 3.76, SD = 10.18. Positive (1,405 trials): downwards n = 835, M = 4.05, SD = 10.98; upwards n = 570, M = 4.48, SD = 10.62 (no asymmetry). (p. 11)

## Simulation scope

### Study 2: simulation scope

`sim-s2`

- **Steps that can be replayed [implementation, to check]**: All screens are text responses: per event the E1 and eBR estimates, BR display, BR recall, E2 and valence rating. E2 for all events is given in Part 2, valence in Part 3. (p. 7)
- **Roles [implementation, to check]**: Every original participant is an AI participant; the experimenter and Qualtrics presentation are scripted only as presentation. No confederates. (p. 7)
- **Conditions [implementation, to check]**: Two between-subject orders (E1 first / eBR first); valence and direction of error are self-classified per trial, not assigned. (p. 7)
- **Outcomes [implementation, to check]**: Update value on neutral-rated trials by direction of error; the paper's LMM is approximated with a random-intercept mixed model. (p. 9)
- **Limits [implementation, to check]**: Blocked until the 51 events and base rates (SI Table S1) and the screen wording are supplied. AI participants answer 51 events with no real exposure to the UK population; Prolific demographics beyond age are not modelled; results are a simulation, not evidence about people. (p. 6)
